● Support SEN

SEN (Semantic Extensions Native) is free and open source, made by a very small team: the semantic desktop for Haiku, and the programs that go with it. If it is useful to you, a small donation helps to keep it going.

Donations go through Liberapay, a non-profit platform for open source. You do not need an account with a code hosting service, and you can give once or every week.
